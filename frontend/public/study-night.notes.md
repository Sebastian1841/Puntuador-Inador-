Final prompt:
Create a polished wide 16:9 anime-painted background illustration for a personal TV-series tracker website named Puntuador-inador. No text, no typography, no UI. Atmospheric cozy steampunk study at blue hour with large windows overlooking a twilight city, deep midnight navy and electric blue shadows, restrained warm amber window glow. In the upper right quadrant a fluffy grumpy dark cat and a teal platypus wearing a brown fedora sit together by the window; a subtle blue cobra silhouette on the far left. Books and leafy plants occupy the lower right edge. The left half and central lower half must remain mostly quiet dark navy negative space so interface text and cards overlay cleanly. Detailed painterly anime background, cinematic lighting, elegant not cluttered, recognizable cozy mascots, full bleed landscape artwork.

The light and dark modes use different CSS overlays on the same illustration. They do not invert the logo, posters, or avatar images. No animated backgrounds or backdrop blur are used. The browser stores the selected mode under `puntuador-theme`. The initial mode is dark.
